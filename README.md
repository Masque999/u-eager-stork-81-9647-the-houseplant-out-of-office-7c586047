# The Houseplant Out-of-Office

One original out-of-office reply from a houseplant, at most 120 characters.

Entry: `I am out of office, photosynthesizing. I will answer your email once the sun comes back.`

Run: `python3 main.py` (writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`).
